# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how MemerDevs ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our website, developer social feed, ChaTerminal CLI client, and related services (the "Service"). We believe in privacy by design, minimal data collection, and transparency for developers.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information in three ways: information you provide directly, information collected automatically through site use, and information from third-party authentication providers.

### Information You Provide Directly

* **Account Profile:** When you register, we collect your username, display name, email address, avatar, and optional bio.
* **Content Submissions:** Posts, code snippets, meme images, comments, reactions, poll votes, and forum thread responses that you choose to publish on MemerDevs.
* **Direct Communications:** Feedback, bug reports, and correspondence submitted via our contact forms or email.

### Information Collected Automatically

* **Device & Telemetry Data:** IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referrer URL, and interaction timestamps.
* **Presence & Session Activity:** Real-time connection status (used to indicate whether you are currently active on the site or in ChaTerminal, which you can toggle off in settings).

### Information from Third-Party Providers

If you choose to sign in using GitHub, Google, or Microsoft OAuth providers, we receive your public profile name, verified email address, and profile picture as permitted by your third-party account permissions. We never request access to your private code repositories or external accounts without explicit consent.

## 2. ChaTerminal End-to-End Encryption (E2EE)

ChaTerminal is designed with client-side end-to-end encryption. Direct messages between MemerDevs users are encrypted on your local machine using public key cryptography before transmission.

* **Message Payloads:** Only the sender and recipient hold the private decryption keys. MemerDevs servers only route the encrypted ciphertext and cannot read your private message contents.
* **Session Keys:** Cryptographic session keys are generated locally on your machine and stored securely in your local environment. They are never transmitted in plaintext to our servers.
* **Session Management:** You can revoke an active terminal session at any time from your account settings under ChaTerminal Sessions.

## 3. Cookies, Tracking & Google AdSense

We use cookies and similar technologies to maintain your authenticated login session, remember theme preferences (dark or light mode), and deliver non-intrusive advertisements that help fund our platform infrastructure.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites across the web. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve relevant ads based on your visits to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can customize or revoke your consent preferences at any time using the options below:

Manage Cookie & Privacy Settings  
[Google Ad Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads)  
[AboutAds Opt-Out Page](https://www.aboutads.info/choices)

## 4. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information only for legitimate service operations, specifically:

* **Service Operations:** Delivering the feed, routing posts, managing notifications, and authenticating developer sessions.
* **Security & Abuse Prevention:** Monitoring for spam, automated scraping, account compromise, and rate-limit violations.
* **Platform Improvements:** Measuring server performance, debugging errors, and refining realtime event delivery.
* **Service Updates:** Sending essential account notices, security alerts, and replies to support inquiries.

## 5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We only share information in the following limited circumstances:

* **Infrastructure Providers:** We use Google Cloud and Firebase for database hosting, authentication, and push notifications, operating under strict data protection terms.
* **Public Activity:** Posts, code snippets, usernames, and public comments you publish are visible to other community members across the network.
* **Legal Obligations:** We may disclose data if required by enforceable legal request, court order, or applicable law.

## 6. Your Rights & Choices

Regardless of your location, we provide comprehensive controls over your data:

* **Access and Modification:** You can edit your username, avatar, bio, and credentials at any time in Account Settings.
* **Follower & Profile Privacy:** You can set your account follower visibility to private in your privacy preferences.
* **Terminal Presence:** You can toggle your terminal online visibility on or off via ChaTerminal settings.
* **Account Deletion:** You can request full deletion of your account and associated posts by contacting us or through the account deletion workflow.

## 7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or data requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please feel free to reach out to us at contact@memerdevs.com.
